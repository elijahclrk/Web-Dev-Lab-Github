Who Can Volunteer?

Anyone passionate about education can join—students, professionals, and community members. Whether you can teach, mentor, or organize, there is a place for you at Agapay. No prior experience is required, just a willingness to help.

Benefits

Volunteering with Agapay allows you to make a real impact on children's literacy while developing your own skills in teaching, leadership, and community building. You will also gain valuable experience, meet like-minded people, and receive a certificate of appreciation for your service.

Reading Buddy

Read with elementary learners and help them practice English in a friendly, encouraging setting. This role is perfect for volunteers who love working with children.

You will be paired with 2–3 learners per session. Each session focuses on guided reading, storytelling, and simple comprehension activities designed by our education team.

Training and reading materials are provided. All you need to bring is patience, warmth, and a genuine love for helping children learn.

About Us

Mission

To empower underprivileged Filipino students by building their English reading and writing skills through volunteer-led sessions, and by directing requested monetary donations toward the cause. Agapay connects volunteers and donors into tangible action that helps students read with confidence, write with clarity, and believe in their own potential.

Vision

A Philippines where every student, regardless of economic background, can read and write in English which will open doors to education, opportunity, and in turn, prepare them to be internationally competent. A foundation where people come together to provide time and necessities to ensure that children are educated, confident, and advanced.

Objectives

Agapay is an initiative with a focused goal: to strengthen English literacy among Filipino youth through volunteer teaching and by responding to requests for monetary support related to the cause. To achieve this, the organization aims to:

  • To provide English reading and writing support to students in low-income communities by mobilizing volunteers to serve as tutors and reading partners.
  • To receive and evaluate requests for monetary assistance related to English literacy such as learning materials, facilities, or student opportunities and direct donations toward fulfilling them.
  • To distribute essential reading and writing tools such as books, workbooks, and school supplies
  • To create a safe and encouraging space for learning, where students feel comfortable practicing English without fear of judgment.
  • To raise awareness about the literacy gap in Philippine education and to inspire others to volunteer or donate.

Company History

  1. 2019

    Agapay started as a weekend reading circle in Metro Manila, where five volunteers tutored twelve children in a low-income community. These early sessions helped students build foundational English reading and writing skills in a safe, encouraging space.

  2. 2022

    Agapay expanded its volunteer network across Luzon and began directing requested donations toward learning materials, books, and school supplies, giving more Filipino students the tools they needed to learn.

  3. 2025

    Agapay launched its official website, connecting volunteers and donors nationwide. This scaled its impact, helping more students read with confidence, write with clarity, and believe in their own potential.
